About

Our Mission & Values

Core Beliefs

We believe Jesus never called believers to casual Christianity.

The Gospel transforms lives.

The Holy Spirit still empowers believers today.

And real discipleship requires surrender, obedience, and spiritual growth.

Our mission is to help people follow Jesus boldly, biblically, and authentically.

Meet Jon and Wanda

Jon and Wanda Ellis are pastors, authors, and leaders passionate about helping believers move beyond surface-level faith into real discipleship.

Through biblical teaching, books, podcasts, and online ministry, they are committed to helping people follow Jesus with boldness, truth, and authenticity.

Our Journey

The Uncommon Christian exists to challenge casual Christianity and call believers into Spirit-led discipleship.

Through sermons, podcasts, books, and digital resources, we equip believers to live boldly, grow spiritually, and follow Jesus without compromise.
